Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidelines regarding unlisted shares, lock-in periods, taxation, and Demat delivery.

Unlisted shares are equity shares of public companies that have not yet listed on public bourses like NSE or BSE. Transactions take place off-market via CDSL/NSDL depository slips and verified scheduled bank escrow accounts, allowing investors to acquire early stakes before the company's IPO.
Yes. You do not need to open a separate Demat account. Any standard CDSL or NSDL Demat account from Zerodha, Groww, AngelOne, Upstox, ICICI Direct, or HDFC Securities works seamlessly.
Under current SEBI regulations, pre-IPO shares acquired by non-promoter retail and HNI investors have a mandatory 6-month lock-in period starting from the official listing date on the exchange.
Under Union Budget 2024 revisions, shares held for more than 24 months are treated as Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) taxed at a flat rate of 12.5% without indexation. Shares held for 24 months or less are treated as Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG) and taxed as per your applicable personal income tax slab.
